## 5. Bounded Fix Log

1. **Gemini Synthesis Contract Alignment (`server.ts` & `meetingContract.ts`):**
   * Configured `responseMimeType: "application/json"` in Gemini synthesis invocation.
   * Added field fallback for divergences (`value.reflection || value.synthesis || value.note`), ensuring resilience across model variations.
2. **Personal Myth Lexical Quality Validation (`server/myth.ts`):**
   * Corrected `карм\w*` regex to `/(?:^|\s)карм(?:а|ы|е|у|ой|ах|ам|ами|ическ\w*)(?:$|\s|[.,!?;:])/iu`. This resolved a major false-positive bug where common Russian narrative words like *"карман"* (pocket) or *"кармашек"* triggered `forbidden_public_language`.
   * Added explicit negative lexical constraints directly to the prompt template to prevent models from generating forbidden therapy/magic jargon.
   * Expanded retry loop to 3 attempts with warning logs.
3. **Automated End-to-End Suite (`scripts/run_g2_acceptance_suite.cjs`):**
   * Implemented full automation covering Route A, Route B, negative inputs, provider faults, feedback errors, keyboard viewport simulation, and synthetic edge cases.
   * Outputs 20 verified screenshots into `docs/evidence/g2-live-acceptance/screenshots/`.
